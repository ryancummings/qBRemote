Control your qBittorrent server from your iPhone or iPad. Simple qBittorrent Remote lets you add torrents, manage transfers, and view download progress.

Features:
- Add magnet links, torrent URLs, and .torrent files.
- Open .torrent files from the Files app.
- Pause, resume, and delete torrents.
- View download and upload speeds, progress, and peer counts.
- Search, filter, and sort your torrent list.
- Move torrents to another folder on your server.
- Organize torrents with categories and tags.
- Switch between saved servers.

The app requires a qBittorrent server with the Web UI enabled. Files download to your computer or network storage device, not to your iPhone or iPad.
