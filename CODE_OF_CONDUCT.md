# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We will make participation in this project free from harassment. We welcome people of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Accept correction and repair harm.
- Protect private information.
- Give credit for other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention, insults, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not allowed in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately through the maintainer contact on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ryancummings). The maintainer will review the report and respond in proportion to its severity.

The maintainer can edit or remove contributions and can temporarily or permanently ban a participant. The maintainer will protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical.

This policy applies to repository spaces and to public representation of the project.
