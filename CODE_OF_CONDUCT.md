# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We will make participation in this project free from harassment. We welcome people of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Accept corrections and address harm that you cause.
- Protect private information.
- Give credit for other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or insult other people.
Do not make sexual comments or advances, publish private information, or repeatedly disrupt project spaces.

## Enforcement

Email conduct problems privately to [support@rads.icu](mailto:support@rads.icu).
The maintainer will review the report and choose a response based on the harm.

The maintainer can edit or remove contributions and can temporarily or permanently ban a participant. The maintainer will protect the reporter's privacy as far as possible.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
